2 Zero-Tolerance User-Generated Content (UGC) Policy

CoMeet enforces a strict Zero-Tolerance Policy against abusive, harmful, or unlawful content in both public live rooms and 1-to-1 video calls.

Strictly Prohibited Conduct:

  • Nudity & Sexually Explicit Acts: Any display of sexual organs, pornography, or solicitation is strictly banned.
