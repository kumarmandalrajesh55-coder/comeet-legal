Privacy Policy

Application Package: com.aistudio.comeet.social • Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Key Privacy Highlights

  • Zero Data Sale: CoMeet never sells, rents, or monetizes personal data to third parties.
  • Real-time Camera/Mic Processing: Video and audio feeds are streamed in real time and are not recorded without user consent.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Payment info is handled by Google Play; CoMeet never stores credit cards.
  • Account & Data Deletion: Full account wipe is available anytime in-app and via our web portal.

1 Introduction & Overview

Welcome to CoMeet ("we", "our", or "us"). CoMeet is a real-time live streaming, 1-to-1 video interaction, and social discovery platform. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information obtained through the CoMeet Android application (com.aistudio.comeet.social) and related online services.

By downloading, registering for, or using CoMeet, you agree to the collection and handling of your data as outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, you must discontinue using CoMeet immediately.

3 Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

CoMeet requests explicit runtime permissions in accordance with Android security best practices. Each permission is requested dynamically only when required:

Android Permission Usage Context Mandatory?
android.permission.CAMERA Capturing video for live broadcasts and 1-to-1 video calls. Camera preview shuts down immediately upon call exit. Required for Video Features
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Transmitting microphone audio during live rooms and video calls. Audio stream stops immediately upon room closure. Required for Voice Features
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Sending push alerts for incoming video calls, chat messages, and live notifications (Android 13+). Optional
android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Allowing users to select and crop a profile avatar from device photos. Optional

4 Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as your account remains active. You maintain full rights to delete your data at any time:

  • In-App Self Service: Open Profile → Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account to permanently erase your profile immediately.
  • Web Deletion Portal: Submit an account erasure request on our dedicated Account Deletion Web Portal.

Upon deletion, all profile data, direct messages, followers, and coin balances are permanently purged from active production databases.
